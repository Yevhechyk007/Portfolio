Psychology / Generative Poster

Inner Noise

An AI-assisted poster exploring mental overload, inner tension, and the contrast between calm external appearance and chaotic internal experience.

The concept focuses on attention as a scarce resource in the age of endless scrolling. Fragmented UI elements, loading states, glitches, and oversized typography create the feeling of being constantly interrupted before a thought can fully form.

The poster uses restraint, noise, and negative space to show how digital systems can look calm on the surface while quietly stealing focus, time, and memory.

FocusAttention scarcity
RoleAI art direction, visual design
ToolsChatGPT
ThemeEndless scrolling, fragmented thinking

Futurism / Generative Poster

City of Tomorrow

An AI-assisted poster imagining a future city where technology, nature, and human harmony grow together.

The concept is built around a hopeful version of urban futurism: architecture becomes organic, infrastructure supports life, and progress is measured by balance rather than speed.

The composition combines exhibition-poster structure with cinematic worldbuilding to frame the city as both an environment and a living system.

FocusNature-driven future cities
RoleAI art direction, visual design

Self-care / Generative Poster

Rebuild Yourself

An AI-assisted poster about burnout, self-acceptance, and the slow process of putting yourself back together.

The poster visualizes recovery as reconstruction. The puzzle pieces represent emotional fragments, while the handwritten notes and inner critic show how healing often happens alongside doubt.

Its warmer palette and softer illustration style create a more intimate tone, turning the poster into a quiet reminder to choose yourself again.

FocusBurnout recovery
RoleAI art direction, visual design
ToolsChatGPT
ThemeSelf-care, balance, reconstruction

Product Campaign / Generative Poster

Matcha Focus Campaign

A two-poster visual campaign built around matcha as clean fuel for focus, energy, and calm productivity.

The series combines bold oversized typography, product-led composition, and a restrained black-green palette to make the drink feel sharp, modern, and performance-focused.

Both posters work as one visual system: one feels like a clean product advertisement, while the second pushes the same identity into a more graphic, close-up campaign direction.
